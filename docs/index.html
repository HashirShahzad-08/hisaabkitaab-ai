Questions

Why does Android ask to allow "unknown apps"?

The app is installed directly from this page instead of the Play Store. Android asks for permission every time you install an app this way. You only need to allow it once, for your browser or file manager.

Is my money data safe?

You sign in with your own email. Your entries are saved in your account, and only you can read them.

Do I need to type in English?

No. Write the way you speak, in Roman Urdu or English, for example "aaj 800 ka petrol". You can also switch the whole app to Urdu in Settings.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. HisaabKitaab AI is available for Android phones.
