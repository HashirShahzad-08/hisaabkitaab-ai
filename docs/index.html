What it does

SMS auto-entry and scam warning

Paste a JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank SMS. It is recorded for you, and scam messages get a clear red warning you can share with family.

Urdu voice entry

Speak with your keyboard's microphone, or type, in Urdu, Roman Urdu or English. Each expense becomes an entry.

Bill Analyzer

Check an electricity bill against the meter readings, understand it in simple words, and get a ready complaint if it looks overbilled.

Can I afford it?

Enter an item and its price. See yes, careful or not now, worked out from your own income and spending.

Health Score

A monthly score from 0 to 100 with a short tip, based on clear rules about saving, spending and budgets.

Full Urdu mode

Every screen works in Urdu script with a right-to-left layout, or in English.

Try it fast

Sign up with an email and password. Then open Settings and tap Load demo data to see a sample month right away.
